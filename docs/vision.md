# Vision

This document captures the early philosophy for Ark World.

The world should feel like a place where people are making life happen: walking to work, trading at the market, repairing a home, learning at a community center, and investing effort into building opportunity.

The role of the player is not to become a hero in a vacuum, but to become someone useful in a growing settlement.

The first version is intentionally smaller than the full vision. The task is to make a compact, compelling world that makes the broader Ark Network idea immediately legible through play.
