# Game Design Document

## Product thesis

Ark World is not a parody of existing life sim games. It is a grounded, opportunity-driven settlement prototype where players move through a small but active world, learn skills, create value, and gradually become useful to a society.

## Pillars

### Play first
The prototype must feel alive and rewarding before it becomes educational.

### Small but coherent world
The initial settlement should be compact but meaningful, not empty or schematic.

### Human-scale systems
The world should feel like people have routines, work, and social life.

### Opportunity over spectacle
The principal movement is from skill to value to contribution.

## Current prototype scope

The first playable slice focuses on:

- third-person movement with sprint, jump, and camera
- NPCs wandering and interacting with the player
- settlement world with basic environment and lighting
- simple skill and economy hooks
- foundation for future quests, dialogue, and property loops

## Example loop

Explore -> Talk -> Learn -> Work -> Produce -> Trade -> Build -> Grow

## Competitive distinction

Ark World’s identity is built on the principle that people solve problems in communities, not just collect resources or complete tasks. The game aims to make value creation feel like the center of play.
